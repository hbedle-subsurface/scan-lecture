The SCAN029 case

From the Californië geothermal wells to the Someren prospect, southeastern Netherlands

Someren license: candidate aquifers are the Cenozoic sands tested in ASTEN-GT-02 (1987): the Breda Formation, the Voort Sand of the Veldhoven Formation, the Basal Dongen Sand and the Houthem Formation, with potential for deeper geothermal below the Cenozoic section.

Californië wells: warm water is produced from the karstified Dinantian carbonate of the Zeeland Formation.
