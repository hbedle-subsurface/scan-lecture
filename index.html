The SCAN029 case

From the Californië geothermal wells to the Someren prospect, southeastern Netherlands

Someren license: the prospect area on the western part of the line, in the Roer Valley Graben. The well inside the license is hidden until the last step of the exercise, so the prospect is explored from the Californië wells.

Someren license: candidate aquifers are the intervals tested in ASTEN-GT-02 (1987): the sands of the 1987 Breda Formation (Oosterhout to Veldhoven formations in the 2021 TNO revision), the Voort Member, the Reusel Member (the 1987 Basal Dongen Sand) and the Houthem Formation, with potential for deeper geothermal below the Cenozoic section.

Californië wells: warm water is produced from the karstified Dinantian carbonate of the Zeeland Formation.
